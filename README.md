## Human Machine Interface <br>
🤖 The course consists of two portions: Industrial Robot Hands-on Practice and Fundamentals of Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs). The industrial robot section includes simulation programming, robot operation, and integration exercises. The AMR section focuses on explaining the theoretical concepts and principles of motion through an exercise kit, the Jetbot, providing students with a solid academic foundation for further research in autonomous robotics.
